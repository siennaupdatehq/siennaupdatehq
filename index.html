ABOUT SIENNA SPIRO

London-born.
One of a kind.

Sienna Ann Spiro is a London-born British singer-songwriter whose music blends pop, soul and jazz with cinematic melodies, piano-led writing and orchestral arrangements. Born on 28 September 2005, she began writing songs at around ten years old and developed an audience online through covers before releasing her own material.

From covers to a breakout

Her first original releases arrived in 2024, including “NEED ME”, “MAYBE.”, “TAXI DRIVER” and “BACK TO BLONDE”. “MAYBE.” became her first major UK breakthrough, while the 2025 EP Sink Now, Swim Later established the emotional, intimate songwriting that would lead into her debut album.

Visitor

Visitor, released in July 2026 through Capitol Records, is Sienna’s debut studio album. The project explores impermanence, love, identity, endings and the feeling of being temporary in somebody else’s life. Its world includes “Die On This Hill”, “You Stole The Show”, “The Visitor”, “Great Expectation”, “Pure”, “He’s Not My Baby, I’m His” and “Mono No Aware”.

Quick profile

Known for her smoky, textured vocal tone and emotionally direct songwriting, Sienna cites jazz and artists such as Amy Winehouse, Frank Sinatra, Adele, Etta James and RAYE among her influences. Her songs often work like miniature films: personal stories built around longing, vulnerability and change.
